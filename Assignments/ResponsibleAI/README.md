# Responsible AI — Ethics and Real-World Impact
### Chrishandra Lewich — ITAI 1371

##  Overview
The Responsible AI assignment focused on understanding the ethical, social, and real-world impacts of artificial intelligence. Instead of coding, this assignment required critical thinking, reflection, and analysis of how AI affects people and communities.

##  What I Did
- Reviewed Responsible AI principles  
- Analyzed real-world examples of AI systems  
- Reflected on fairness, bias, transparency, and accountability  
- Answered questions about ethical decision-making  
- Connected course concepts to real-life situations  

##  What I Learned
- Why fairness and transparency matter in AI  
- How bias can appear in datasets and models  
- Why responsible design is essential for safe AI systems  
- How AI impacts different groups of people  
- How to think critically about ethical challenges in technology  

##  Challenges
The main challenge was thinking beyond the technical side of AI and focusing on human impact. This assignment helped me understand that responsible AI is just as important as accurate AI.

##  Summary
The Responsible AI assignment strengthened my understanding of ethics in technology. It helped me think more deeply about how AI systems affect people and why responsible design is essential for building trustworthy machine learning models.
